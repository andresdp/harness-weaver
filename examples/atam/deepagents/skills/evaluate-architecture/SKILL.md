# Evaluate Architecture with ATAM

Read the bundled `references/atam-methodology.yaml` and `references/atam-report-template.json` before analysis.

1. Establish scope, business drivers, architecture evidence, constraints, and open questions.
2. Refine quality attribute drivers into scenarios with stimuli, environments, responses, measurable response measures, and priority judgments when evidence supports them.
3. Identify architecture approaches only from supplied evidence. Mark inference explicitly.
4. Analyze prioritized scenarios against architecture decisions. Record risks, non-risks, sensitivity points, tradeoff points, rationale, and evidence.
5. Synthesize the report using the bundled `ATAM_Evaluation` template. Put generated findings under `evaluation_results`, preserve gaps and unknowns, and do not invent stakeholder workshop outcomes or measurements.
6. Ask the evidence reviewer to check method coverage and claim support before returning the report for architect review.
