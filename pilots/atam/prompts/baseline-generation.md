# Revfactory/harness prompt-based baseline

Build a harness that applies ATAM (the Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method)—a structured way to assess how an architecture supports business goals and prioritized quality-attribute scenarios, and to identify the resulting risks, sensitivities, and trade-offs—to the supplied banking case by examining concerns such as security, reliability and recovery, performance, consistency, and ease of change, grounding conclusions in the case evidence and making missing banking-specific information explicit.
