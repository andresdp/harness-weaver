## Harness: SARA architecture design

**Goal:** Apply the SARA four-stage process to a supplied project description and functional requirements and produce a traceable architecture decision (`architecture_decision.json` with ADR and Mermaid diagram) plus a register of assumptions and unresolved gaps.

**Invocation conditions:** When asked to derive, design, evaluate or recommend a software architecture, or to produce an ADR/architecture diagram from requirements — including re-runs, partial redos of a stage, and fixes to previous results — use the `sara-orchestrator` skill. Simple questions about the process or existing outputs may be answered directly.
